Welcome to Paris

Paris, the capital city of France, is known as the “City of Light”. It is admired worldwide for its art, architecture, fashion, and café culture. The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and the Seine River are just a few of its iconic landmarks.

Famous Attractions

From the romantic streets of Montmartre to the historical charm of Notre-Dame Cathedral, Paris offers endless exploration. Stroll along the Champs-Élysées or take a cruise on the Seine to experience the city’s timeless beauty.

Art and Culture

Paris is home to some of the world's greatest museums and galleries. The Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, and Centre Pompidou display masterpieces that shaped art history. Its theaters and opera houses continue to inspire audiences globally.

French Cuisine

Parisian food culture is world-famous — from buttery croissants and fresh baguettes to fine dining and Michelin-starred restaurants. Savor the flavors of France with every bite.
